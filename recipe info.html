Chicken Biryani

A high-protein, low-calorie chicken biryani packed with lean chicken, fragrant spices and fluffy rice.

Ingredients

  • 200g Chicken Breast
  • 150g Basmati Rice
  • Greek Yoghurt
  • Onion
  • Biryani Spice Mix
  • Fresh Coriander

Cooking Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken.
  2. Cook the rice.
  3. Cook the chicken.
  4. Combine everything together.
  5. Serve hot.
